# ZILFIT Shoe Design Language V1.0

**Date:** 2026-05-27
**Status:** Visual direction & engineering constraints — no final CAD

---

## 1. Design Philosophy

ZILFIT shoes project **medical futuristic trust**. The silhouette
communicates precision, data-awareness, and engineered comfort.
It avoids athletic flash, streetwear noise, and clinical sterility.

Five principles:
1. **Clean silhouette** — No unnecessary seams, panels, or decorative stitching
2. **Data-visible** — Capsule compartment is exposed, not hidden
3. **Soft geometry** — Rounded transitions, no sharp edges on wearer contact
4. **Breathable shell** — Perforated upper, lattice-ventilated sole
5. **Removable layers** — User can swap top layer without discarding shoe

## 2. Silhouette Direction

```
         ┌──────────────────────┐
         │   Breathable upper   │
         │   (perforated TPU    │
         │    coated textile)   │
H        │                      │         T
E   ┌────┤                      ├────┐    O
E   │ Capsule                  Capsule│  E
L   │ window                   window │
    │                                │
    │   ░░░░ lattice midsole ░░░░░   │
    └───█████████ outsole █████████──┘
```

- **Heel:** Elevated slightly (8–12mm), rounded basin contour
- **Midfoot:** Visible lattice structure, capsule port on medial side
- **Forefoot:** Wide toe box, flexible segmentation lines
- **Collar:** Padded knit, ankle-friendly, no Achilles pressure

---

## 3. Heel Geometry

- Deep heel cup (12–15mm wall)
- Medial and lateral containment wings
- Internal density map printed in PA11
- No rigid heel counter — compliance from lattice density

---

## 4. Outsole Philosophy

- Two-part: printable lattice midsole + bonded rubber outsole pads
- Contact zones: heel strike, lateral midfoot, met heads, hallux
- Tread: minimal wave pattern, no aggressive lugs
- Color: vantablack base, metallic accents (rose gold or silver)

---

## 5. Breathable Shell Strategy

- Upper: TPU-coated polyester mesh, laser-perforated
- Ventilation channels aligned with thermal vent lattice in sole
- Toe cap: reinforced but perforated
- Tongue: integrated, no separate piece

---

## 6. Removable Top Layer

- Insole/liner is **not glued** — user-removable
- Can be swapped for different densities, textures, or sensor pads
- Attachment: 3 locating pins in heel + forefoot (snap fit)
- Allows: cleaning, replacement, upgrading to Smart Capsule version

---

## 7. Hidden Electronics Compartment

- Location: Medial midfoot, between lattice and upper
- Access: Slide-open cover, tool-free
- Contents: Smart Capsule PCB, battery, vibration module (future)
- Cable routing: Internal channel from compartment to 4 sensor pads
- Water resistance: IPX4 rated compartment seal

---

## 10. Non-Clinical Boundary

This design language describes **aesthetic and engineering intent**
only. No therapeutic, medical, or corrective function is implied by
the form language. The visual design supports trust and comfort
perception without making health claims.
