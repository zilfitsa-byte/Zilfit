## Safety Stop Conditions

**STOP IMMEDIATELY AND REMOVE SHOES IF:**
- Sharp pain or pinching sensation
- Audible cracking or snapping sound
- Feeling of instability or loss of balance
- Visible crack, split, or delamination
- Outsole pad becoming loose or detached
- Any electrical sensation (if electronics are active)
- Skin breaking, bleeding, or blistering

## Post-Test Inspection

After removing shoes:

1. **Shoe exterior:** Check all shell seams, outsole bond lines, and lattice visibility
2. **Shoe interior:** Check liner condition, skin surface for cracks
3. **Liner:** Remove and inspect for compression set or tearing
4. **Weight re-check:** ___________ g (left), ___________ g (right)
   - Any weight loss > 5g may indicate material loss (fracture, abrasion)
