📱 iPad Safari Capture Guidance
▾ Show
01Use HTTPS — getUserMedia requires a secure origin. Serve via ngrok, Tailscale, or local HTTPS tunnel.
02Hold camera perpendicular to floor — arm straight down, ~40–60 cm above foot. Tilt adds nonlinear scale error.
03Card and foot on same flat plane — both on the floor or table surface. If card is elevated, the scale ratio will be wrong.
04Show full card boundary — all 4 corners of the ID/bank card must be visible. The auto-detector looks for the outer rectangle.
05Show heel and longest toe — foot must be fully in frame. Do not cut off the heel or toes.
06Limit lower leg in frame — keep the ankle and below only. Too much leg confuses foot boundary detection.
07Even lighting — no harsh shadows across foot or card. Shoot near a window with diffuse light or use a ring light.
08Light background — place foot on white paper or light floor for better contrast. Dark foot on dark mat = detection failure.
⚠ Known Limitations — R&D Reference
▾ Show
✕Perspective distortion — camera not perfectly perpendicular introduces nonlinear scaling. No depth correction in 2D-only mode.
✕Card misalignment — if the reference card is not co-planar with the foot, the px/mm ratio is incorrect and all measurements are invalid.
✕Shadows across card or foot — shadow edges are mistaken for object boundaries, shifting bounding box edges.
✕Foot not flat — raised toes or lifted heel underestimate actual foot length. Measure under full bodyweight standing conditions ideally.
✕Heuristic detection only — no ML model. Edge/contrast heuristics fail on complex or dark backgrounds. Manual box is always more reliable.
✕Single-camera 2D — arch height, 3D width, and instep girth cannot be measured from a single overhead image.
✕Must validate against manual tape measurement — all outputs from this prototype require ground-truth comparison before use in any sizing or manufacturing workflow.
